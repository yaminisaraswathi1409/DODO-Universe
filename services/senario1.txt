DODO-UNIVERSE

Scenario 1 — Dynamic Order, Transport, Driver & Recovery Requirements

Ordered Functional Requirements / Business Rules


1. PURPOSE

Scenario 1 defines a flexible order-to-delivery workflow in which the customer first orders a product or service, then declares what transport resources they already have or want to arrange.

The accepted service provider completes any missing vehicle or driver arrangements.

Transport incidents, mechanics, spare parts, and labour are added only when actually needed.

The same architecture must support many products, services, vehicle types, providers, and future resource types without hardcoding a single workflow.


2. CORE OPERATING PRINCIPLE

Customer orders the product/service first.

Customer records whether they already have a vehicle and/or driver, or want those resources arranged.

Only approved/eligible providers who opted into the requested service should receive the matching order.

Before acceptance, the provider sees the real order details and can Accept or Decline.

After acceptance, the provider becomes responsible for fulfilment and for arranging only the missing resources.

Breakdown, mechanic, spare-parts, loading, and unloading resources are optional and triggered only when required.


3. CUSTOMER BOOKING — VEHICLE CHOICE

During booking, show exactly these three options:

1. I will book a vehicle
2. I have a vehicle / Myself
3. I do not have a vehicle

If the customer chooses "I have a vehicle / Myself", show a dynamic/admin-configurable vehicle form.

Suggested core fields:

- Vehicle Type
- Vehicle Registration Number
- Vehicle Owner Name
- Vehicle Contact Number

Admin may add other vehicle fields such as:

- Model
- Capacity
- Vehicle Photo
- RC/Document
- Insurance
- Other business-specific information

Required/optional status, labels, field types, and validation must be configurable.


4. CUSTOMER BOOKING — DRIVER CHOICE

During booking, show exactly these three options:

1. I will book a driver
2. I have a driver / Myself
3. I do not have a driver

If the customer chooses "I have a driver / Myself", show a dynamic/admin-configurable driver form.

Suggested core fields:

- Driver Name
- Driver Phone
- Driving Licence Details — when required
- Driver Photo — when required
- Other Admin-configured information

Admin must be able to add, remove, or configure driver fields without hardcoding a separate form for each service.


5. VEHICLE + DRIVER COMBINATION RULES

Vehicle and driver are independent resources.

The platform must support any valid combination of the three vehicle choices and three driver choices.

Customer vehicle choice:
I have a vehicle / Myself

Customer driver choice:
I have a driver / Myself

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider uses customer-supplied vehicle and driver; no additional booking required.


Customer vehicle choice:
I have a vehicle / Myself

Customer driver choice:
I do not have a driver

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider sees vehicle details and arranges a driver.


Customer vehicle choice:
I do not have a vehicle

Customer driver choice:
I have a driver / Myself

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider sees driver details and arranges a vehicle.


Customer vehicle choice:
I do not have a vehicle

Customer driver choice:
I do not have a driver

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider arranges both vehicle and driver.


Customer vehicle choice:
I will book a vehicle

Customer driver choice:
I will book a driver

Result after provider acceptance:
Customer places the service order; after acceptance, provider completes both bookings.


Customer vehicle choice:
I will book a vehicle

Customer driver choice:
I have a driver / Myself

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider arranges vehicle; customer-provided driver is used.


Customer vehicle choice:
I have a vehicle / Myself

Customer driver choice:
I will book a driver

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider arranges driver; customer-provided vehicle is used.


Customer vehicle choice:
I will book a vehicle

Customer driver choice:
I do not have a driver

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider arranges both vehicle and driver.


Customer vehicle choice:
I do not have a vehicle

Customer driver choice:
I will book a driver

Result after provider acceptance:
Provider arranges vehicle; driver is handled through the selected/required driver arrangement.


6. CUSTOMER ORDER SUBMISSION

The customer should complete the product/service order before transport execution begins.

The order captures:

- Requested service
- Exact quantity
- Delivery address
- Service-specific details
- Vehicle choice
- Driver choice
- Customer-provided vehicle information, when applicable
- Customer-provided driver information, when applicable

If the customer selected "I will book a vehicle" or "I will book a driver", the customer does not need to complete that booking before submitting the service order.

The accepted service provider can arrange the missing resources afterward.


7. PROVIDER MATCHING AND ACCEPTANCE

After the customer submits the order, the system should identify users/providers who are already approved and opted into the requested service/category and who satisfy the existing matching rules.

Before acceptance, the provider must see accurate data from the actual order record:

- Service/category
- Exact quantity
- Required date/time
- Delivery location
- Pickup/source location, when applicable
- Dynamic service-specific details
- Customer vehicle choice and customer-provided vehicle details, when supplied
- Customer driver choice and customer-provided driver details, when supplied

The provider must have clear actions:

[Accept Order]
[Decline]

Only after acceptance does the order move into the provider's "My Jobs".

Declined providers do not receive the order in My Jobs, and the request remains available to other eligible providers.


8. INFORMATION AVAILABLE AFTER PROVIDER ACCEPTANCE

Only the provider who accepted the order should receive the operational contact information necessary for fulfilment.

The provider should be able to see:

- Customer name
- Customer phone number
- Relevant pickup/delivery location
- Customer-provided vehicle details, when supplied
- Customer-provided driver details, when supplied

The provider can contact the customer to coordinate:

- Pickup date/time
- Delivery timing
- Vehicle arrival
- Driver arrival
- Other necessary details


9. MISSING VEHICLE / DRIVER ARRANGEMENTS

After acceptance, the service provider fills only the missing transport resources.

Customer has vehicle + no driver
→ Provider arranges/books driver.

Customer has no vehicle + driver
→ Provider arranges/books vehicle.

Customer has neither
→ Provider arranges/books both vehicle and driver.

Customer has both
→ No unnecessary vehicle/driver booking is created.

Vehicle and driver remain independently bookable.

A truck owner may also be the driver or may have a separate driver.

A provider may book one resource while using the other resource supplied by the customer.


10. DYNAMIC VEHICLE AND DRIVER SERVICES

Vehicle and driver services must use the existing dynamic service/category architecture.

Admin should be able to configure vehicle types and driver requirements without code changes.

Vehicle examples:

- Truck
- 10-wheeler
- 12-wheeler
- Trailer
- Mini truck
- Tractor
- Future vehicle types

Driver services may be offered as an independent service/resource.

Vehicle and driver fields must be dynamically configurable.


11. LOADING AND UNLOADING LABOUR

The logistics workflow must also support optional loading and unloading labour.

These are separate resources that may be requested by the customer or accepted service provider when needed.

Loading labour can be requested at the pickup/source location.

Unloading labour can be requested at the destination.

Labour quantity, timing, location, eligibility, provider matching, and charges must be dynamic.

Do not automatically create labour requests when they are not needed.

The responsible party for labour charges must be explicit and shown separately.


12. TRANSPORT EXECUTION

Once the required vehicle/driver resources are available, the transport workflow can proceed through:

- Dispatch
- In-transit monitoring
- Delivery

The exact resource steps should depend on the choices made and the requirements of the service.


13. BREAKDOWN / EMERGENCY REPAIR

A mechanic workflow starts only after a genuine transport problem is reported.

Driver, truck owner, or accepted service provider may report the vehicle problem.

The reporter can upload:

- Issue photos
- Video
- Problem description
- Current location

The issue evidence must be visible to the truck owner and Admin for trust/audit purposes.

Nearby eligible mechanics can be found using the existing location/PostGIS matching.

A mechanic can accept the repair request and resolve the issue.

After repair, the journey resumes and continues toward delivery.


14. SPARE PARTS PROCUREMENT

If the mechanic determines that a spare part is required:

Driver, truck owner, or accepted service provider can search nearby automobile/spare-parts providers in DODO-Universe.

If an appropriate part is available in the app, it can be requested/purchased through the platform.

If no suitable part/provider is available in the app, the driver/provider may obtain it offline.

Offline procurement must not block repair or delivery completion.

The event/cost can still be recorded for audit and settlement.


15. COST RESPONSIBILITY

Product/service price
Responsible party:
Customer / service order


Agreed transport charge
Responsible party:
As defined for the order; customer pays only when transport is part of the customer-facing arrangement


Driver charge when provider arranges driver
Responsible party:
Service provider, as applicable to fulfilment


Mechanic / repair charges caused by truck problem
Responsible party:
Truck/vehicle owner


Spare-part charges caused by truck problem
Responsible party:
Truck/vehicle owner


Loading/unloading labour
Responsible party:
Configured responsible party; shown separately


If driver pays an emergency cost first
Driver records proof and requests reimbursement from the responsible vehicle owner.


The customer should not be unexpectedly charged for truck breakdown, mechanic, or spare-parts costs.

Customer billing should remain limited to:

- Agreed product/service price
- Applicable transport/delivery charges
- Applicable GST/taxes


16. BREAKDOWN TRUST, EVIDENCE AND NOTIFICATIONS

Notify the responsible truck owner when a breakdown, repair, or spare-part cost is raised.

Notify Admin for visibility and audit.

Store uploaded photos/videos and supporting information against the relevant transport/repair workflow record.

Record:

- Who reported the issue
- Who booked the mechanic
- Who booked the spare part


17. LOCATION-BASED MARKETPLACE RESOURCES

The marketplace must support location-based provider/resource discovery.

Registered members can publish what they currently offer within a configurable radius.

Example:

A bricks provider can advertise:

200 bricks available within 50 km.

A transport provider can advertise:

An available vehicle within a defined radius.

Customers/providers see suitable nearby offers based on location.

Search and ranking should use:

- Location
- Existing matching rules

Radius must be configurable.

Quantity and availability must come from real provider data.

Do not hardcode:

- Radius
- Inventory
- Provider identity
- Vehicle type
- Availability

The same model must support:

- Materials
- Vehicles
- Drivers
- Mechanics
- Spare parts
- Loading labour
- Unloading labour


18. DYNAMIC ARCHITECTURE RULE

Treat the following as dynamic resources/services:

- Vehicle
- Driver
- Transport
- Mechanic
- Spare Parts
- Loading Labour
- Unloading Labour

Each resource can be:

- Introduced
- Configured
- Approved
- Matched
- Booked
- Billed

without creating a separate hardcoded workflow.


19. TARGET END-TO-END WORKFLOW

1. Customer selects a product/service and exact quantity.

2. Customer confirms delivery address and service-specific details.

3. Customer selects Vehicle:
   - I will book
   - I have vehicle
   - I do not have vehicle

4. Customer selects Driver:
   - I will book
   - I have driver
   - I do not have driver

5. If customer provides vehicle/driver, the configured details are captured dynamically.

6. Customer submits the order.

7. Approved/eligible providers who opted into the requested service receive the matching request.

8. Provider reviews the real order data and chooses:
   - Accept
   - Decline

9. After acceptance, provider receives the required customer, vehicle, driver, and location details for fulfilment.

10. Provider arranges any missing vehicle/driver resources.

11. Optional loading/unloading labour is added only when required.

12. Transport begins.

13. If a breakdown occurs, driver/owner/provider reports the issue with evidence.

14. Nearby mechanic is matched; spare parts are obtained in-app or offline if required.

15. Repair is completed and transport resumes.

16. Delivery is completed and proof is recorded.

17. Customer receives the agreed invoice without unexpected truck-repair costs.

18. Provider/truck-owner/driver settlements are recorded separately according to responsibility.


20. IMPLEMENTATION RULES FOR THE EXISTING CODEBASE

Before implementing any part, inspect the existing:

- Database
- Migrations
- APIs
- Matching system
- Transport system
- Driver system
- Repair system
- Parts system
- Labour system
- Dynamic configuration

Reuse existing structures whenever possible.

If a feature is already implemented, keep it and fix only what is necessary.

If a feature is partially implemented, complete the missing portion instead of rebuilding it.

Do not create duplicate:

- Tables
- APIs
- Order systems
- Provider systems
- Dynamic-field systems

Use the database/backend as the source of truth.

Do not use hardcoded:

- Quantities
- Provider names
- Vehicle types
- Prices
- Locations
- Service-specific fields

Do not create:

- Transport workflows
- Driver workflows
- Mechanic workflows
- Parts workflows
- Labour workflows

unless the corresponding resource is actually required.
